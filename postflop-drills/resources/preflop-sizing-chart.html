Adjustments

  • Limpers: add 1bb to your open for each limper.
  • Squeeze (open plus callers): your normal 3-bet plus one open-size for each caller. At 30bb and below, squeeze all in.
  • Out of position, go bigger: 3-bet about 4× from the blinds vs 3× in position, so the caller pays more to play the hand with position.
  • Short stack behind you: at 20–25bb, prefer a min-raise you can fold to a re-jam, or open-jam, over a big open you are committed to.
  • Effective stack: size for the smaller stack in the hand, not your own.

Why sizes shrink as stacks shrink

  • Opens stay about 2–2.3bb at every depth; what changes is the 3-bet.
  • A 3-bet is a share of your stack: 7bb is 9% of 80bb but 23% of 30bb. Once a 3-bet costs a fifth of your stack, you can't fold to a jam, so the solver jams instead.
  • That is why the all-in share jumps from about 0% at 50bb to about a third at 30bb and most 3-bets at 20bb.
  • 4-bets go all in from about 50bb: a 4-bet to 15bb leaves too little behind to fold.

Opens, SB sizes and all-in shares: your PokerCoaching charts. Your charts don't list 3-bet and 4-bet sizes, so the bb amounts are standard sizes (the 50bb solver spots use 6.6bb in position and 8.8bb from the blinds).
