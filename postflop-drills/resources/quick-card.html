Bubble quick card

Chips lost cost more than chips won are worth. Required equity = pot odds + risk premium.

Top mistakes

  1. Calling 3-bets with small pairs at 40bb or less. Fold or jam them: set-mining needs about 15–20 times the call behind, and at 50bb you have about 6× (Figure 4). Call opens with them only in position or in the big blind.
  2. Stacking off with one pair when 150bb+ deep. Keep pots small with one pair deep; get it in with two pair or better.
  3. Opening the same hands at every depth. Swap small pairs and small suited connectors for offsuit Broadways as you get shorter (Figure 2).
  4. Making 3-bets you can't call a jam with, at 20–35bb. Re-jam or flat instead; small 3-bets with medium hands get shoved on.
  5. Min-raising at 15bb, then calling off light. Shove the hands you don't want to face a re-jam with; min-raise only hands happy to call or easy to fold.
  6. Calling jams as wide as you would shove. Calling needs a hand ahead of their range; your calling range should be clearly tighter than your shoving range.
