How to read each page

Charts: the preflop raiser's range on the left (red) and the caller's range on the right (green). Partly filled cells are hands played only some of the time; the fill shows how often.

Range advantage goes to whoever's whole range has 53% or more equity against the other's on that flop (shown as raiser – caller equity). Between 47% and 53% is Even.

Nut advantage compares how much of each range is in the strongest 5% of hands on that flop (the cutoff hand is in brackets). One side needs about 1.3 times the other's share, and at least 1.5 points more, to have the edge.

The flop ranges are the solver's exact starting ranges for each spot (preflop chart ranges with the flop cards removed). Ranges come from your PokerCoaching charts; solutions from postflop-solver.
